SANTA — private dictation and transcription for your Mac
=======================================================

Santa turns speech into text on your own Mac. Nothing is sent to the cloud:
no account, no subscription, no internet needed once it is installed.
Languages: English, Hindi, Arabic, Hinglish, Bengali (plus auto-detect).


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
* A Mac. Best on Apple Silicon (M1–M5); Intel Macs work but are slower.
  Tested on macOS 26.
* About 5 GB of free disk space.
* Internet during installation and the first start (it downloads Python and
  the speech models, about 2 GB, once).
* No administrator password: everything installs in your own user account.


INSTALL (10–15 minutes, mostly downloading)
-------------------------------------------
1. Unzip this file and move the "Santa" folder to Documents.
   Keep it there; Santa runs from this folder.

2. Double-click "Install Santa.command".
   macOS will probably say it "could not verify" the file. That is normal for
   anything downloaded that isn't from the App Store. To allow it:
     System Settings › Privacy & Security › scroll down ›
     "Install Santa.command was blocked" › Open Anyway › Open.
   (Or open Terminal and drag "Install Santa.command" into it, then press Return.)

3. A Terminal window shows the progress. When it says "INSTALL OK", Santa
   opens in your browser. The first start downloads the speech models, so
   wait until the status at the top says the model is ready.

That's it. You can close the Terminal window.


USING SANTA
-----------
Two ways, same engine:

1) THE SANTA WINDOW   (Spotlight ⌘ Space › "Santa")
   * Pick the language you will speak, press Start recording, speak, press
     Stop. The text appears and you can edit, copy, read aloud or export it.
   * Output: "As spoken", or translated to English or Arabic (offline; the
     original is always kept).
   * Files & links: drag in audio/video files (interviews, meetings), paste a
     file path, or paste a link (YouTube, Loom, a public Google Drive file).
     Tick "Separate speakers" to get "Speaker 1 / Speaker 2" transcripts.
   * Settings › Words Santa should know: names and terms it should spell right.
   * Settings › Transcript files & watch folder: save .json + .srt transcripts
     automatically, or watch a folder and transcribe every new video dropped
     into it.

2) THE FLOATING CAPSULE   (Spotlight › "Santa Assistant")
   A small bar that floats above all windows. Click into any text box (email,
   WhatsApp, Word…), then click the capsule's mic, speak, click again: the text
   is typed where your cursor is.
   Or hold fn + Control while speaking and let go. A quick tap of fn + Control
   keeps it listening until you tap fn again.
   Right-click the capsule for language, output, start at login, restart, quit.

   The first time, macOS asks for three permissions. Allow all three for
   "Santa Assistant" (System Settings › Privacy & Security):
     Microphone · Accessibility (to type the text) · Input Monitoring (fn key)
   Then right-click the capsule › Restart Santa Assistant.

The first time you use translation or speaker separation, Santa downloads
those models (about 100 MB per language pair, 35 MB for speakers), once.


PRIVACY
-------
* Audio is processed on this Mac and deleted straight after; it is never
  stored. Transcript history is OFF unless you switch it on.
* Saved voices (for recognising people in interviews) stay on this Mac and
  can be deleted in Settings. Ask people before saving theirs.


IPHONE (not needed now — kept for later)
---------------------------------------
Santa can also be used from an iPhone on the same Wi-Fi. It is OFF by
default and nothing is reachable from your phone or network until you switch
it on in Settings › iPhone access. The steps are in
docs/santa/USER_GUIDE.md, section 9.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
* Full guide: docs/santa/USER_GUIDE.md (troubleshooting in section 8).
* Logs (they never contain your words): ~/Library/Application Support/Santa/logs
* To remove Santa: double-click santa-app/Uninstall Santa.command. It asks
  before deleting each part, then you can delete this folder.

Santa is built on Whisper Local (MIT licence, see LICENSE) and OpenAI's
Whisper speech models.
