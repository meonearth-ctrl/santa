Santa for Windows: Interviewer Guide

Santa turns your interview recordings into transcripts on your own laptop. Videos never leave the computer. You upload the transcript to the Hiring Right Kout app, which does the scoring.

1 · Install (once, about 10 minutes)

  1. Plug in the laptop and stay on the internet: the speech model (about 1.6 GB) is downloaded once.
  2. Double-click Santa-Setup.exe. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. No administrator password is needed.
  3. Keep all the ticks and click Install. A black window shows the model download; let it finish.
  4. Santa opens in your web browser and a Santa icon appears in the taskbar next to the clock (click ^ if you don't see it). From now on Santa starts by itself when you sign in.

2 · After each interview

  1. Name the video date_city_group, e.g. 2026-11-30_KOL_G1.mp4 (mp4, mov, m4a and wav all work, up to 120 minutes).
  2. Copy it into Documents › Hiring Right › 02_Batch_Videos.
  3. Wait. Santa starts once the copy has finished. On a normal laptop a 60-minute video can take 30–60 minutes; with an NVIDIA graphics card it is much faster. Keep the laptop plugged in. Santa stops it from sleeping, but don't close the lid.
  4. Open Documents › Hiring Right › 03_Transcripts. You will find two files with the video's name: .json (upload this to the Hiring Right Kout app) and .srt (the same text with times, for reading).
Shortcut: right-click the Santa tray icon to open the Videos or Transcripts folder. To see progress, click Open Santa. You can also drag a video onto the Santa page; the same files are saved.

3 · If something goes wrong

No transcript appearsIs the Santa icon in the tray? If not, open Santa from the desktop or Start menu, then wait.
A file .error.txt appearsOpen it: it says why (e.g. no sound, file too long). Fix it and copy the video in again.
"Model is downloading"The first start downloads the model if set-up couldn't. Connect to the internet once.
Very slowClose other heavy apps. Several videos are done one after another, not at once.
Antivirus blocks SantaAsk IT to allow %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Santa.

4 · Good to know

  • Private: after set-up Santa works offline. Audio, video and text stay on this laptop.
  • Santa never moves or deletes your videos. Delete them yourself once all raters have scored, as Hiring Right asks.
  • The transcript is in English. Speaker labels ("Speaker 1"…) are a guess; Hiring Right matches candidates by the number they say.
  • Need about 4 GB of free disk space.
  • Remove Santa: Settings › Apps › Santa (offline transcription) › Uninstall. Your Hiring Right folder is kept.
