Santa for Windows - offline transcription for Hiring Right interviews

1. Unzip this whole folder (right-click > Extract All).
2. Double-click "Install Santa.bat". If Windows says "Windows protected your PC",
   click "More info" > "Run anyway". No administrator rights are needed.
3. Keep the internet on during installation: the speech model (0.5-1.6 GB) is
   downloaded once. After that Santa works offline.

Santa then runs in the taskbar tray and watches
   Documents\Hiring Right\02_Batch_Videos
Copy an interview video there; the transcript (.json + .srt) appears in
   Documents\Hiring Right\03_Transcripts
Upload that .json into the Hiring Right Kout app.

To remove Santa: double-click "Uninstall Santa.bat" (or use the Santa-Setup .exe
installer's entry in Settings > Apps if you installed it that way).
Full guide: "Santa for Windows - Interviewer Guide" next to this download.
